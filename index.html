Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 28, 2026

Orbit is a personal AI assistant. We prioritize your privacy by design.

1. Data Collection

Orbit does not create accounts. We do not collect personal identification information (PII) such as names or email addresses.

2. How Data is Handled

  • On-Device Mode: When using local models, all prompts and responses stay on your device. No data is transmitted to any server.
  • API Mode: When you configure an external API endpoint, your prompts are sent directly to that provider. Orbit does not intercept, store, or see this data.

3. Local Storage

Your conversation history and settings are stored locally on your device. You have full control over this data and can clear it at any time within the app.

4. Third Parties

We do not share any data with third parties. We do not use analytics or tracking SDKs.

5. Changes to this Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by the "Last Updated" date above.
